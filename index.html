Smart Schedule

Smart Schedule helps the Construction Services industry plan the day without any confusion.

That is the project’s intended benefit. Benefits not yet validated.

The problem

Construction Services scheduling brings together job requests, technician qualifications, availability, and travel between sites. Smart Schedule gives contractors a place to submit requests and schedulers a workspace to coordinate assignments.

The goal is clearer daily planning. User research and measured improvements have not yet been supplied.

How it works

From a contractor’s request to a technician’s scheduled visit, the prototype follows three steps.

  1. STEP 01

    Submit a job request

    The contractor enters project details, location, requested date, and service requirements.

  2. STEP 02

    Match a technician

    The prototype checks certifications, availability, and travel requirements, then ranks eligible technicians using travel and workload. Requests without an eligible match stay pending for review.

  3. STEP 03

    Review the schedule

    The scheduler reviews the assignment and its explanation, then can reassign or move the visit, subject to certification and scheduling checks.

Demo & features

Working features

  • Contractor request intake. A dedicated workspace collects project details, requested dates, and service requirements.
  • Automatic technician matching. Eligibility checks consider certifications, availability, schedule conflicts, and travel gaps. Eligible candidates are ranked using travel estimates and workload, with contractor history and preference also considered.
  • Scheduler review and override. Assignment explanations and a schedule view let schedulers review visits, reassign technicians, and adjust the schedule.

These features are present in the local prototype source. Their presence does not establish real-world performance or validated benefits.

Evidence & limits

Benefits not yet validated.

No test results, performance measurements, or user-study evidence have been supplied.

  • Status: class-project prototype.
  • Matching: automatic assignment uses scheduling rules and weighted scoring. Travel estimates exclude live traffic; offline fallback estimates are labeled.
  • Local prototype: records are saved on one computer; separate computers do not synchronize automatically. The role selector does not provide password-based authentication.
  • This website: an introduction only; it does not provide AI scheduling.
  • Demo evidence: the team-provided screen recording illustrates the prototype; it does not establish measured performance or validated benefits.
